## ✅ 1. Cybersecurity Introduction

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks.  
These attacks aim to access, change, or destroy sensitive information, disrupt services, or steal money.

---

## ✅ 2. Importance of Cybersecurity

- Prevents data breaches and financial loss  
- Maintains trust and reputation of companies  
- Protects national and organizational security  
- Ensures business continuity  

### 🔥 Real-World Consequences of Cyber Attacks:

- Loss of customer data
- Company shutdowns
- Ransomware demands
- Legal penalties and fines

---

## ✅ 3. Proven Strategies Organizations Use to Prevent Cyber Attacks

| Strategy                | Description                                                                 |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Regular Security Checks| Identify vulnerabilities via scanning, audits, and patching regularly       |
| Advanced Monitoring    | Real-time detection of threats using tools like SIEM                        |
| Employee Training      | Teaches staff to avoid phishing and weak security practices                 |
| Data Encryption        | Scrambles data to make it unreadable to attackers                          |
| Incident Response Plan | Step-by-step process to respond to and recover from cyber incidents         |

---

## ✅ 4. Core Components of Cybersecurity (The "Cybersecurity Triangle")

| Component | Description                                                                 |
|----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| People   | Employees and users; must be trained and aware of security best practices  |
| Process  | Defined workflows, rules, and policies for secure operations               |
| Technology | Tools and systems (like firewalls, antivirus, SIEM) used for protection |
